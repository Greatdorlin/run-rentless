# Cohort qualification and pricing

Public pages explain the guided AI-building programme but do not display a price.
The report form saves the chosen engagement through the existing Sender fields.

Owner-provided starting prices for private conversations: USD 500 minimum;
Nigeria starting price NGN 500,000. Confirm participant count, scope and fees
before quoting. Do not infer nationality or offer eligibility solely from an IP.

Do not calculate savings by subtracting an invented replacement cost from bills.
The audit has no verified build, migration, support or running-cost estimate.
Only discuss a savings-linked fee after agreeing the savings period and checking
all costs. The proposed "half the savings" offer is not an automated quote.

No fixed percentage saving, overnight delivery or production safety guarantee
is made. Complex or sensitive systems may require specialist review.
